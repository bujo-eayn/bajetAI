# bajetAI

> An Agentic AI-Powered Platform for Enhancing Public Participation in Public Matters

---

## Overview

**bajetAI** is a web-based AI platform that simplifies complex public-interest documents and enables meaningful citizen engagement. It supports **governments, civil society organizations, institutions, and other bodies** to interact effectively with the public.

The platform provides:

* AI-powered summarization and translations of large documents
* Public engagement via feedback submission and conversational Q&A
* AI-assisted analysis of public feedback to inform evidence-based decisions

---

## Challenges in Public Participation

Public engagement is often hindered by long, technical documents, language barriers, and large volumes of unstructured feedback. Organizations often lack tools to efficiently synthesize this input into actionable insights.

**bajetAI addresses these challenges** by enabling:

* **Citizens** to understand and interact with documents easily
* **Organizations** to efficiently analyze feedback with AI, including sentiment, topic, and area-of-interest summaries

---

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Document Accessibility

* Upload and manage public-interest documents (budgets, policies, plans, reports)
* AI-generated summaries in **English and Swahili**
* Automatic translations between English ↔ Swahili
* Full document viewing with integrated PDF readers

### 2. Public Engagement

* Open public access (no login required for reading)
* Submit public feedback on documents
* Conversational interface to **chat with documents**
* Structured participation across thematic areas

### 3. Feedback Intelligence (For Organizers)

* AI-powered analysis of public feedback
* Automatic summarization of large volumes of feedback
* Sentiment analysis (positive, neutral, negative)
* Topic and theme extraction
* Highlighting key areas of interest or concern

This ensures decision-makers are **not overwhelmed** by feedback while maintaining diverse public perspectives.
